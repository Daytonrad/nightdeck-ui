# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, whatever someone's experience, background or identity.
- Give and take feedback kindly and focus on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, sexualised language or personal attacks, in public or private.
- Do not share other people's private information.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, report it privately through GitHub: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, and mark the report as a conduct issue. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues, and may block people who break this code of conduct.
